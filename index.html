About Sachin Mehra

A fusion of engineering rigor, curious mindset, and passion for the microscopic world of semiconductor silicon.

From Silicon Sand to High-Performance Architecture

I am a 1st-year B.Tech undergraduate studying Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) with a focused specialization in Semiconductor and Chip Design at JECRC University, Jaipur.

My passion lies at the very bedrock of modern computation: how billions of microscopic transistors collaborate to execute complex instructions in picoseconds. I am deeply interested in VLSI design, embedded systems, and computer architecture, with a clear aspiration towards semiconductor manufacturing and IC verification.
